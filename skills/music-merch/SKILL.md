# Music Merch

Plan, produce, price, and sell merchandise.

## Profile
Read `~/.claude/skills/music/artist-profile.json` first. Use `identity` to infer what sells for this genre/audience.

## Strategy
When to launch merch (decision framework). What sells by genre (ambient → art prints/tote bags, hip-hop → hoodies/hats, indie → enamel pins/lyric prints, etc.). Limited drops vs permanent store. Bundle strategy (merch + music, merch + ticket).

## Production
Print-on-demand (Printful, Printify, Spreadshirt — web search current pricing by region). Custom manufacturing for items POD doesn't cover. Artwork brief template with specs. Quality checklist — always sample first.

## Store setup
Bandcamp merch, Shopify (when it makes sense), Instagram/TikTok shop. Pricing formula: production cost × 3, adjusted by genre expectations.

## Drops
Announcement plan: teaser → reveal → launch → last chance. Limited edition mechanics. Collaborator merch (revenue/credit split).

## Post-launch
Sales tracking table. Restocking decision framework.

## After generating
Suggest: `/music-finances` for income tracking, `/music-live` for merch at shows, `/music-fans` for superfan drops.
