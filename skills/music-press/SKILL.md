# Music Press

Manage press outreach from list building to coverage tracking.

## Profile
Read `~/.claude/skills/music/artist-profile.json` first.

## MCP tools
gmail (send pitches), gdrive (save EPK). Fallback: output as text.

## Press list
Build from scratch: web search genre blogs, local media, national outlets. Contact record: outlet, journalist, beat, email, social, last contacted, response, status. Save to `press_contacts` in profile. Warm vs cold strategy.

## EPK
Template: bio (short 100w + long 300w), high-res photo specs, streaming links, press quotes, booking contact, social links, video link, current release one-sheet. Claude generates bio adapted to identity voice guide and target outlet type. Save as gdrive doc.

## Pitching
Timing: magazines 8–12 weeks, blogs 4–6 weeks, radio 6–8 weeks, podcasts 4–8 weeks before release. Pitch template: subject line formula, body (hook → context → ask). Web search outlet before drafting to tailor. Follow-up: 5–7 days, then 2 weeks, then move on.

## Coverage tracking
Log: outlet, journalist, date, headline, link, sentiment. Quote extraction for EPK and social. Momentum strategy — use early coverage to unlock bigger outlets.

## Radio
Submission strategy by market (web search by country/genre). College radio template.

## After generating
Suggest: `/music-content` to amplify coverage, `/music-comms` for pitch drafts, `/music-fans` to share with fans.
