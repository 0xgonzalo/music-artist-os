## Templates

### Collaboration agreement
Two types:
- **Split agreement** (co-writers/co-producers): ownership %, credit, licensing decisions, administration
- **Work-for-hire** (session musician, mixer): flat fee, no ownership, credit terms

### Producer agreement
Points of production, advance/fee, royalty split, credit format, stems delivery, exclusivity period.

### Sync licensing agreement — key clauses
- **Exclusivity**: can you license elsewhere?
- **Territory**: where it airs
- **Term**: how long (1 year? perpetuity?)
- **Fee structure**: upfront vs backend vs both
- **Credit**: on-screen and metadata

### Management deal red flags
- Commission above 20%
- Commission on pre-deal income
- Sunset clause missing or too long (should be 1–2 years)
- No "key person" clause
- Includes master/publishing ownership
- Unclear or one-sided termination

### Recording contract checklist
- Who owns masters?
- Advance amount and recoupment mechanics?
- How many albums/options?
- Royalty rate and calculation method?
- Territory and term?
- What happens if label folds?

## Important

After every template or legal discussion, always say:

> **This is a reference template only. Have a music lawyer review any agreement before signing.** To find one, search for entertainment or music attorneys in your area — many offer initial consultations at reduced rates.
