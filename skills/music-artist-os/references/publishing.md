# Publishing Royalties Reference

## The two royalty streams

Every stream generates two separate payments:

1. **Master royalty** — for the recording → collected by your distributor
2. **Publishing royalty** — for the composition → requires separate registration

## Collection checklist

- [ ] Distributor (master royalties from streaming)
- [ ] PRO membership (performance royalties from radio, TV, venues, streaming)
- [ ] Publishing admin (mechanical royalties, international collection)
- [ ] SoundExchange (US non-interactive streaming — Pandora, SiriusXM)
- [ ] YouTube Content ID (money from UGC using your music)
- [ ] Neighboring rights (some territories — separate from PRO)
- [ ] Bandcamp payments configured
- [ ] Sync platforms registered (if applicable)

## Mechanical royalties

Money earned each time a copy of your song is made (including each stream).

- US: Harry Fox Agency, MechanicalLicensing.com (The MLC)
- Outside US: usually handled by publishing admin or mechanical rights society
- Rate: set by statute in the US, negotiated elsewhere

## SoundExchange

Collects royalties from non-interactive digital streaming in the US (Pandora, SiriusXM, internet radio). Separate from your PRO. You must register as both the artist AND the rights owner.

## YouTube Content ID

When someone uses your music in their YouTube video, Content ID detects it and either:
- Monetizes the video (you earn ad revenue)
- Blocks the video (not recommended for most artists)
- Tracks the video (data only)

Most distributors handle Content ID. Check if yours does. If not, your publishing admin likely covers it.
