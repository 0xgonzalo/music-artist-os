# Music Fans

Build an audience you own.

## Profile
Read `~/.claude/skills/music/artist-profile.json` first.

## MCP tools
gcal (newsletter schedule), gmail (fan communications). Fallback: output as text.

## Mailing list
Why email is the most valuable asset (owned, algorithm-proof, highest conversion). Platform comparison (web search current pricing): Mailchimp, Substack, ConvertKit, Beehiiv, MailerLite. List building: Bandcamp, show merch table (QR code), pre-save gates, link in bio, website. Welcome sequence: 3-email flow adapted to identity. Cadence: monthly minimum, bi-weekly during releases. Content ideas and subject line formulas.

## Community platforms
Discord: channel structure, roles, keeping it active. Patreon/membership: tier structure and pricing (web search norms). Bandcamp subscribers: exclusive content ideas. Telegram/WhatsApp: when they make sense (small/intimate).

## Superfan strategy
Identify superfans (buy everything, share, show up, engage, reply). Exclusive offers: early access, limited merch, private sessions, name in credits. Fan data capture: QR at shows, pre-save gates, content gates.

## Fan communication templates
All adapted to identity voice: release announcement (exclusive angle), show announcement, milestone thank-you, re-engagement email.

## Long-term
Consistent touchpoints between releases. Fan-to-fan dynamics. Graduating fans: listener → follower → subscriber → buyer → superfan.

## After generating
Suggest: `/music-merch` for superfan drops, `/music-analytics` for list metrics, `/music-content` for content driving signups.
